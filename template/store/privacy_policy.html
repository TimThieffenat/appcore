__APP_NAME__ ("the app") is __DESCRIPTION__ for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects nothing automatically; an error report reaches us only if you decide to send it by email.

Data we collect

Nothing automatically. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it has no permission to use the internet. We do not store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

Your data

Everything you create or save in the app is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app erases it.

Error reports (optional)

To help fix problems, the app keeps a technical log on your device: what it did (pages opened, settings changed, sound start-up), app errors, and, on Android 11 and later, the reason Android gives when the app stopped (crash, not responding, low memory). The log is size-limited, stays in the app's private storage and is erased when you uninstall the app or press Clear in Settings › Error report.

The log is sent only if you choose to: Send by email opens your own email app with the report filled in, addressed to the developer. You can read or edit it, and nothing is sent until you press Send. A report contains the app version, your phone model and Android version, the app settings (language, theme, note names, instrument), error messages and the recent log. It contains no audio, no contacts and no location. Like any email, it shows your email address to the developer. Reports are used only to fix the app, are not shared with anyone, and are deleted once the problem is solved. You can ask for a report to be deleted at the address below.

Permissions

The app does not use the microphone, the camera, your location, your contacts or your files. Its only permission is the "modify audio settings" permission that the sound library uses to play sound, which Android grants without asking.

Network

The app does not need an internet connection, has no permission to use it, and does not send data over the network. Error reports go through your email app, only when you send them.

Children

The app collects no data automatically from anyone, including children.
